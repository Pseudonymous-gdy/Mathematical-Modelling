# Practical Plan for Problem 1 (BN/DBN + VI + PL Head + True-Rule Replay)

## Goal and High-Level Pipeline
**Goal.** Estimate weekly fan vote shares (and scaled vote totals) for each active couple, while (i) being consistent with DWTS's official elimination rules and (ii) quantifying uncertainty.

**Core idea.** We use a *Bayesian dynamic graphical model (DBN)* with two latent processes: **Skill** ($X$) and **Popularity** ($Z$). Judges' scores are emitted only from $X$, while fan votes are driven jointly by $(X,Z)$. The show’s official composite score is applied to $(J,p^F)$ to produce a rule-defined ordering. For *training*, we attach a differentiable **Plackett--Luce (PL) tail-likelihood head** on the composite-score ordering. For *evaluation*, we do not use PL; instead we *simulate* eliminations under the **true rule** using posterior samples (*posterior predictive replay*).

## Indices and Observations
### Indices
- $s\in\{1,\dots,S\}$: season index ($S=34$);
- $t\in\{1,\dots,T_s\}$: week index within season $s$;
- $i\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}$: couple index in the active set $\mathcal{A}_{s,t}$.

### Observed data
- Judges' total score:
  $$J_{i,t}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{J}_{s,t}}\text{score}_{i,t,j}.$$
- Elimination outcome (typically single elimination):
  $$\mathcal{E}_{s,t}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{s,t},\quad |\mathcal{E}_{s,t}|\in\{0,1,>1\}.$$

### Preprocessing (recommended)
To avoid over-parameterized intercepts, standardize judges' totals within each week:
$$\tilde J_{i,t}=\frac{J_{i,t}-\mathrm{mean}(J_{\cdot,t})}{\mathrm{sd}(J_{\cdot,t})+\epsilon}.$$
(We will still compute $P^J_{i,t}$ and $R^J_{i,t}$ directly from raw $J_{i,t}$ for the official rule.)

## Latent Variables and Their Meaning
### Latent states (DBN core)
- $X_{i,t}$: **Skill / professional performance state** (drives judges' scores);
- $Z_{i,t}$: **Popularity state** (audience preference, also influenced by skill).

### Fan vote (target)
$$p^F_{i,t}=\frac{F_{i,t}}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}}F_{k,t}},\qquad \sum_{i\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}}p^F_{i,t}=1.$$
Absolute vote totals are non-identifiable; we report scaled totals $\widehat F_{i,t}=\widehat V_t\,\mathbb{E}[p^F_{i,t}\mid\mathcal D]$ using a chosen $\widehat V_t$.

## DBN Core: State Dynamics and Emissions
### (I) Skill Chain: depends only on itself
$$b_{i,X}\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_{bX}^2),\qquad X_{i,1}\mid b_{i,X}\sim\mathcal N(b_{i,X},1),$$
$$X_{i,t}\mid X_{i,t-1},b_{i,X}\sim\mathcal N(\phi_X X_{i,t-1}+b_{i,X},\sigma_X^2),\quad t\ge2.$$

### (II) Popularity Chain: depends on itself and skill
$$b_{i,Z}\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma_{bZ}^2),\qquad Z_{i,1}\mid b_{i,Z}\sim\mathcal N(b_{i,Z},1),$$
$$Z_{i,t}\mid Z_{i,t-1},X_{i,t-1},b_{i,Z}\sim \mathcal N(\phi_Z Z_{i,t-1}+\beta_{XZ}X_{i,t-1}+b_{i,Z},\sigma_Z^2),\quad t\ge2.$$

### (III) Judges' scores: emitted only from skill
$$\tilde J_{i,t}\mid X_{i,t}\sim \mathcal N(X_{i,t},\sigma_J^2).$$
This emission anchors $X_{i,t}$ with strong observations.

### (IV) Fan votes: emitted from popularity and skill
Define a vote-utility:
$$A_{i,t}=Z_{i,t}+\lambda_{ZX}X_{i,t}+\delta^\top x_i,$$
where $x_i$ are static covariates (optional; add later with shrinkage).
Fan vote shares are generated by:
$$p^F_{i,t}=\frac{\exp(A_{i,t})}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}}\exp(A_{k,t})}.$$
**Implementation note (identifiability):** we do not include a week bias term in $A_{i,t}$ because softmax is invariant to adding a constant to all $A_{\cdot,t}$.

## Official Composite Score (Mechanism Layer)
We preserve the show-defined rule; vote inference affects elimination only through $p^F_{i,t}$.

### Percent rule (Seasons 3--27)
Compute judges' percentage:
$$P^J_{i,t}=\frac{J_{i,t}}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}}J_{k,t}},$$
and composite score:
$$S^{\text{percent}}_{i,t}=P^J_{i,t}+p^F_{i,t},\qquad \text{(smaller $S$ = higher risk).}$$

### Rank rule (Seasons 1--2, 28--34): training vs replay
**Hard rank (used in replay/evaluation):**
$$S^{\text{rank}}_{i,t}=R^J_{i,t}+R^F_{i,t},\qquad R^F_{i,t}=\mathrm{rank}(p^F_{i,t}).$$
**Training-time differentiable proxy (used only for gradients):**
$$\widetilde R^F_{i,t} =1+\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t},\,k\neq i}\sigma\!\left(\frac{p^F_{k,t}-p^F_{i,t}}{\rho}\right),\quad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}},$$
$$\widetilde S^{\text{rank}}_{i,t}=R^J_{i,t}+\widetilde R^F_{i,t}.$$
**Practice rule:** fix $\rho$ as a small constant or anneal $\rho\downarrow 0$; do not learn $\rho$.

## PL Head for Training (Single Global Temperature)
### Why PL head
The observed supervision is a *tail event* of the ordering induced by the official composite score. PL provides a differentiable listwise tail likelihood without turning the problem into a separate classifier.

### Risk attractiveness (same scale across rules)
Define a raw risk score $r_{i,t}$ (larger $r$ means more likely to be eliminated):
$$r_{i,t}= \begin{cases} -\;S^{\text{percent}}_{i,t}, & \text{percent rule (small $S$ is worse)},\\ +\;\widetilde S^{\text{rank}}_{i,t}, & \text{rank rule (training)}. \end{cases}$$
To make the risk scale comparable across rules/weeks, standardize within each week:
$$\hat r_{i,t}=\frac{r_{i,t}-\mathrm{mean}(r_{\cdot,t})}{\mathrm{sd}(r_{\cdot,t})+\epsilon}.$$
Then a **single** inverse-temperature $\tau$ is sufficient for all seasons:
$$p(e_{s,t}\mid \hat r_t,\tau)= \frac{\exp(\tau \hat r_{e,t})}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{A}_{s,t}}\exp(\tau \hat r_{k,t})}.$$
For $|\mathcal{E}_{s,t}|>1$, use PL sampling without replacement over the tail (or marginalize permutations if unordered and small $m$).

## Variational Inference (Training)
Let latent variables be $\mathcal{Z}=\{X_{i,t},Z_{i,t}\}$ and parameters be $\Theta=\{\phi_X,\sigma_X,\phi_Z,\beta_{XZ},\sigma_Z,\lambda_{ZX},\delta,\sigma_J,\tau\}$ (with priors).

### Joint model factorization
$$p(\tilde J,\mathcal E,\mathcal Z,\Theta)= p(\Theta)\,p(\mathcal Z\mid\Theta)\,p(\tilde J\mid X,\Theta)\,p(\mathcal E\mid \hat r(\tilde J,p^F),\tau).$$

### Variational family
Choose a reparameterizable approximate posterior $q(\mathcal Z,\Theta)\approx p(\mathcal Z,\Theta\mid \tilde J,\mathcal E)$ (e.g., mean-field Gaussian for $\Theta$ and structured Gaussians for time series states).

### ELBO objective
Maximize
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ELBO}} = \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(\tilde J\mid X,\Theta)] + \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(\mathcal E\mid \hat r,\tau)] - \mathrm{KL}\big(q(\mathcal Z,\Theta)\,\|\,p(\mathcal Z,\Theta)\big).$$
Optimization uses stochastic gradients with the reparameterization trick.

## Evaluation by True-Rule Posterior Replay (Consistency & Certainty)
PL is *not* used in evaluation. We evaluate using the true DWTS rule via posterior predictive replay.

### Posterior replay procedure
For $m=1,\dots,M$ posterior samples:
1. Sample $(X^{(m)},Z^{(m)},\Theta^{(m)})\sim q$ and compute $p^{F,(m)}_{i,t}$.
2. Apply the **true rule** to obtain composite scores:
   - percent seasons: $S^{\text{percent},(m)}_{i,t}=P^J_{i,t}+p^{F,(m)}_{i,t}$;
   - rank seasons: $S^{\text{rank},(m)}_{i,t}=R^J_{i,t}+R^{F,(m)}_{i,t}$ with hard ranks.
3. Simulate elimination under the true mechanism to get $\mathcal E^{(m)}_{s,t,\text{sim}}$.

### Consistency metrics
Compute expected replay accuracy:
$$\mathrm{Acc}=\frac{1}{\sum_{s,t}\mathbb{I}(|\mathcal E_{s,t}|=1)} \sum_{s,t:\,|\mathcal E_{s,t}|=1} \mathbb{E}\Big[\mathbb{I}\big(\mathcal E^{\text{sim}}_{s,t}=\mathcal E^{\text{obs}}_{s,t}\big)\Big],$$
and optionally Top-$k$ coverage or season-wise breakdown.

### Certainty metrics
Use posterior uncertainty in vote shares:
$$\mathrm{CIwidth}(p^F_{i,t}) \ \text{and}\  \mathrm{Entropy}(p^F_{\cdot,t}),$$
as well as replay variability $\Pr(\mathcal E^{\text{sim}}_{s,t}=i)$ over posterior samples.

## Implementation Checklist (Team Execution)
1. Parse weekly active sets $\mathcal A_{s,t}$ and eliminations $\mathcal E_{s,t}$; compute $J_{i,t}$, $P^J_{i,t}$, $R^J_{i,t}$.
2. Preprocess $\tilde J_{i,t}$ (within-week standardization).
3. Implement DBN prior and emissions: $(X\to \tilde J)$ and $(X,Z\to A\to p^F)$.
4. Implement mechanism layer: compute $S^{\text{percent}}$ or $\widetilde S^{\text{rank}}$ (training), and compute $r,\hat r$.
5. Implement PL head likelihood with a single $\tau$.
6. Train with VI by maximizing ELBO.
7. Evaluate with posterior predictive replay under **true** rules, report consistency and certainty.
